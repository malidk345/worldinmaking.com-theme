⚡ Bolt: [performance improvement] Optimize notification refresh pipeline

What:
- Removes redundant 45-second polling interval in `useUser.tsx`.
- Removes redundant `fetchUser()` call when opening the notifications panel.
- Refactors `useUser.tsx` to use a debounced `syncNotifications` function.
- Skips redundant `supabase.auth.getSession()` calls by passing known `userId`.

Why:
- The previous implementation triggered excessive and redundant network requests when opening the panel, focusing the window, and via a background polling timer.
- Local assistant notifications were merged twice in two different places.

Impact:
- Significantly reduces backend load and improves client-side performance.
- Eliminates overlapping fetch race conditions.

Measurement:
- Removed a `setInterval` of 45s.
- Avoided 1 `auth.getSession()` per notification fetch.
- Replaced multiple renders with unified deduplication.
