⚡ Bolt: [performance improvement] Replace iterable spreading + reduce with for...of loops

💡 What: Replaced `[...iterable].reduce(...)` patterns in `markdown.ts` and `documentModel.ts` with `for...of` loops.
🎯 Why: Using array spread `[...iterable]` on Sets or strings before calling `.reduce()` creates temporary intermediate arrays and adds callback overhead, leading to unnecessary GC pressure and CPU cycles during markdown parsing and model updates.
📊 Impact: Eliminates temporary array allocations and callback overhead in `getListItemDepth` and `mapNodeInlineChildren`, providing a small O(N) execution optimization.
🔬 Measurement: Check the updated files; verify that performance regressions are not introduced via `pnpm run test:smoke`.
